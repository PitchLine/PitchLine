How Pitchline works

01

Build a real profile

Players list position, stats, highlight clips and history. Academies list facilities, coaches and open positions. Under-18 profiles require a parent or guardian on the account.

02

Get matched on fit

Our matching system surfaces players and academies based on position, age group, location and playing style — not on who's paid for a higher tier.

03

Request contact, safely

Coaches send a trial or contact request instead of a phone number. For minors, the request routes to their parent or guardian to accept, decline, or reply.

Built around child safety, not around it.

✓

No public contact details for minors

Phone numbers and addresses are never shown on a young player's profile. All contact runs through Pitchline's request system.

◐

Parent/guardian verification

Every player under 18 is linked to a verified parent or guardian account, who must approve any trial or contact request.
